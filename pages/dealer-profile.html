About Baypoa Motors

Baypoa Motors is one of Nairobi's most trusted used car dealerships, established in 2017 and based in Westlands. We specialise in quality Japanese imports, locally used vehicles, and direct imports from the UK and UAE.

Our team of experienced automotive professionals ensures that every vehicle in our inventory is thoroughly inspected, properly documented and fairly priced. We offer flexible financing options through partnered banks and SACCOs.
